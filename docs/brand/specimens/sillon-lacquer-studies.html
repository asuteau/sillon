Lacquer studies

Each study swaps only the three lacquer stops and the text colour on them. The chrome stays black, white and grey. The fake covers in each mock are there to test how the lacquer sits next to records of every hue.

Stops are drawn at 120deg with the middle stop at 60%, same as --lacquer today. Text on lacquer is checked for at least 4.5:1 against the darkest stop. The icon and splash use the same gradient on the groove stroke, as GrooveMark tone="lacquer" does.
