Sillon · design system · copper middle stop

#b06d4a or #ba7a55

Copper master, with only the middle stop changing. Left is the original, deeper stop. Right is the lightened one. Buttons are Satin, so the middle stop only touches their edge and shadow. Most of the difference shows in the marks and the sleeves.

Sleeve grounds are the fixed composition colours from house-sleeve.ts. The contrast figures are for the middle stop against each ground, which shows how much the darkest part of a lacquer shape stands out. 3:1 is the WCAG threshold for graphics.
