Button studies in gold and copper

Nine ways to paint the lacquer button, on the same Gold disc and Copper master stops. Each one is shown on a light and a dark app background, so the theme toggle isn't needed. Hover the buttons: each style has its own hover. Copper's middle stop is lightened from #b06d4a to #ba7a55, which gives 5.6:1 with the label instead of 4.7.
